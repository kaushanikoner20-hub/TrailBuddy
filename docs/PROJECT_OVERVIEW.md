# Project overview

## Problem
People often want to spend time outdoors but stay tied to their screens. "Go outside" is easy advice and hard to act on, and planning an outing usually means more scrolling.

## Target user
Anyone who wants a simple, low-pressure reason to go outside for 15 minutes to 90 minutes, whatever their fitness level.

## Solution
TrailBuddy asks how you want to feel and how much time you have, then uses a local AI model to design a short adventure made of sensory missions: listen, look closer, notice, remember. You read it once and leave.

## Philosophy
TrailBuddy is an AI designed to make itself unnecessary.

> What if the best thing an AI could do for you was make you stop looking at it?

The AI prepares the experience; it doesn't follow you outside. The interface is meant to get simpler as you go: personalize, generate, read, leave. It avoids chat, feeds, notifications, and statistics.

## Why sensory missions
The point is not to say "walk for 30 minutes". Missions are small observation challenges that help you notice your surroundings, so the time outside is the experience rather than a task.

## Why it exists
TrailBuddy is a small open-source project for the Hacktoberfest 2026 "Touch Grass" challenge.

- **Stage 1:** foundation and local Gemma connection (done).
- **Stage 2 (current):** personalized, structured, sensory adventures.
- **Stage 3 (planned, not built):** the "Are you going out?" moment, closing scene, and Phone Away Mode.
- **Stage 4 (planned):** local saving and offline use.
